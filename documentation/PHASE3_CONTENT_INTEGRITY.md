# PACE ITS — Content Integrity & Fact Preservation Policy

1. **Zero Fabrication Policy:** All faculty names, HOD messages, intake numbers, placement offers, committee compositions, and accreditation claims are derived verbatim from `pace.ac.in`.
2. **Department Independence:** Every one of the 13 Engineering & PG departments and 7 Polytechnic sections contains its own specific text, tables, and syllabi.
3. **Document Authenticity:** All 4,608 PDFs link to authentic official university files.
